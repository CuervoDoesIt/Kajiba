<objective>
Create `src/kajiba/eval_scorer.py` — a deterministic completeness/confidence scorer for
`ExperimentRecord` (EEVAL-01). It mirrors `scorer.py`'s structure (WEIGHTS dict, threshold
constants, `compute_*` entrypoint, frozen-shape result dataclass) but uses eval-native
sub-checks and distinct band vocabulary (`complete`/`partial`/`thin`). It is a trust lens
on the eval RECORD (is it complete enough to analyze?), NOT a re-judgment of the model's
answer — the existing `eval_score` stays the answer-quality signal (D-01).

are complete and trustworthy. Compute-on-read (D-03) — never persisted, schema stays frozen.
