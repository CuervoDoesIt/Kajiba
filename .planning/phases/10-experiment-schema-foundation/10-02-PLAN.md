<objective>
Refactor `src/kajiba/schema.py` to extract a shared `RecordBase`, add the `record_kind` discriminator, introduce the `ExperimentRecord` family (`ExperimentMetadata`, `ExperimentOutcome`, `ExperimentRecord`), add a manual `load_record()` dispatch factory, and bump `SCHEMA_VERSION` to `0.2.0` — all WITHOUT perturbing `KajibaRecord`'s hash methods, `validate_record()`, or `ConversationTurn`.

Purpose: This is the schema foundation for the dual-use v1.2 milestone. It is, per RESEARCH and PATTERNS, almost entirely *replication of existing idioms in this same module* onto new fields — not invention. The dominant risk (byte-identical legacy hashes, ESCH-04) is retired by leaving `KajibaRecord.compute_record_id/compute_submission_hash` byte-for-byte unchanged; the second risk (legacy-dict dispatch) is retired by a manual factory, NOT a discriminated union.

Output: A refactored `schema.py` containing `RecordBase`, `KajibaRecord(RecordBase)`, the three new experiment models, three new dual vocabularies, the `load_record()` factory, an unchanged `validate_record()`, and `SCHEMA_VERSION = "0.2.0"`.
</objective>

<locked_technical_decisions>
These are RESOLVED by SPEC/CONTEXT/RESEARCH — implement as-is, do NOT re-open:
- **Loader = manual factory**, NOT a discriminated union. `load_record(data)` branches on `data.get("record_kind", "coding_session")`. A `Field(discriminator="record_kind")` union is FORBIDDEN — it raises `union_tag_not_found` on every legacy dict (which omits `record_kind`). VERIFIED in RESEARCH.
- **`validate_record()` stays byte-for-byte unchanged** (`-> KajibaRecord`, body `return KajibaRecord.model_validate(data)`). No call-site changes anywhere.
- **`KajibaRecord.compute_record_id()` / `compute_submission_hash()` bodies stay byte-for-byte unchanged.** Only their class *location* (now a subclass) changes. They hash explicitly enumerated fields, not `model_dump()` — moving fields to `RecordBase`, adding `record_kind`, and bumping `SCHEMA_VERSION` cannot perturb output (RESEARCH proved byte-identical for a real fixture).
- **Experiment id hash (D-04):** reuse the existing idiom (`json.dumps(..., sort_keys=True, ensure_ascii=True)` → SHA-256) over experiment-identity content = `experiment_id` + `task_description` + `local_model.model_name` + `outcome.local_model_output` + `started_at`. Serialize `started_at` with `.isoformat()` (locked here per RESEARCH Assumption A1 / Pitfall 4 — `datetime` is not JSON-serializable). Prefix `kajiba_exp_<first 12 hex>` (D-05).
- **`ExperimentRecord.compute_submission_hash()` IS computed** (not `None`), format `sha256:<hex>`, over the same experiment-identity content — purpose is LOCAL dedup, not community dedup (D-06).
- **`eval_score: float`** uses `Field(ge=0.0, le=1.0)` (mirrors `composite_score`, RESEARCH Assumption A2).
- **Scalar Literals** (`record_kind`, `experiment_type`, `recommended_action`) need NO `field_validator` — Pydantic enforces membership automatically for scalar Literal fields. Still define the companion `tuple` per the dual-vocab convention. (A `field_validator` is only needed for `list[Literal]` fields like `outcome_tags`.)
- **`compute_*` per-subclass, NOT abstract on base** — the two payloads share nothing.
- **`model_config = {"populate_by_name": True}` lives ONCE on `RecordBase`**; subclasses inherit it. Do NOT re-declare a partial/empty config on `KajibaRecord` or `ExperimentRecord` (Pydantic overrides wholesale, not deep-merge — Pitfall 3).
- **`validate_turn_count` / `validate_tool_call_counts` stay on `KajibaRecord`** — they reference `self.trajectory` (KajibaRecord-required). Do NOT move them to base or attach to `ExperimentRecord` (Pitfall 5).
</locked_technical_decisions>
